Part 1: Use Risk Analysis Methods

Step 1: Calculate the Asset Value

Initial cost: $20,000 Organizational value: $40,000 Public value: $75,000

What is the total asset value of the server?

$20,000 + $40,000 + $75,000 = $135,000 (tangible and intangible value combined).

Why is the intangible cost so high? Is this realistic?

Yes, it is realistic. A business’s reputation and customer trust take years to build and are hard to restore once damaged. A compromised customer database can mean lost customers, lost revenue, and liability costs that far exceed the price of the hardware.

Step 2: Calculate the Exposure Factor

Asset value: $135,000 Restoration cost (flood): $30,000

Exposure Factor: $30,000 / $135,000 = 0.22 (22%)

Step 3: Calculate the Single Loss Expectancy

SLE = Asset Value × Exposure Factor

What is the SLE (hard drive failure, EF 0.5)?

$135,000 × 0.5 = $67,500

SLE of a ransomware attack (EF 1.0)?

$135,000 × 1.0 = $135,000

Step 4: Calculate the Annualized Rate of Occurrence

ARO = 12 months / months between events

a. ARO of a ransomware attack (every 24 months)?

12 / 24 = 0.5

b. ARO of hardware failures (every 30 months)?

12 / 30 = 0.4

Step 5: Calculate the Annualized Loss Expectancy

ALE = SLE × ARO

ALE of a hardware failure (SLE $5,000, ARO 2.5)?

$5,000 × 2.5 = $12,500

ALE of a hacking attack (SLE $10,000, ARO 0.5)?

$10,000 × 0.5 = $5,000
